Activity Haven is a recreation centre for adults 50+ in Peterborough, offering 80+ drop-in and instructional programs, five days a week — cards, crafts, fitness, music, and friends.

Who we are

A centre run by the community, for the community

A centre that is proactive, progressive, accountable and innovative, providing recreational services to the 50+ population in Peterborough and surrounding areas. Involvement, innovation, integrity, transparency and inclusiveness are foundational to every aspect of the centre.
