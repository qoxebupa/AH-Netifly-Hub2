Who We Are

Who we are

Activity Haven is a social hub where members aged 50 and older can connect with peers, pursue shared interests, and enjoy activities together. A place to belong, every single day.

Our story

Activity Haven became an incorporated organization in April 1990, operating independently under the supervision of a Board of Directors. By 1994, infrastructure funding enabled facility expansions, including accessibility improvements for people with disabilities. Today the centre runs more than 80 ongoing programs at the Queen Alexandra Building — from card games to dance, woodcarving, martial arts, and daily yoga classes — supported by roughly 200 volunteers across every part of the operation.

Vision

A centre that is proactive, progressive, accountable and innovative, providing recreational services to the 50+ population in Peterborough and surrounding areas.

Mission

To promote socialization of adults 50+ with a view to support and strengthen the determinants of health and wellbeing.

Values

Involvement, innovation, integrity, transparency and inclusiveness are foundational to every aspect of the centre.
